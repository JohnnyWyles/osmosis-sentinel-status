About this page

Rate-limit use is the share of each asset's binding window already used: net flow against the rate-limiter contract's cap. Alerts fire at 80%, 95% and 100%, where transfers are blocked. Snapshots are taken every 15 minutes.

Governance tallies are live while voting is open. The outlook is how the vote would resolve if it closed now: quorum on turnout of bonded stake, then veto, then yes against the non-abstain votes. The white mark on the turnout bar is quorum.

Pegs quote selling $10,000 of each USD stable or BTC variant into allUSDC or allBTC along the best route; a rate 1% (USD) or 2% (BTC) away, or no route at that size, is flagged with its cause: the asset's alloy is full (its share is at the transmuter's static limit, so it can't be swapped in), thin liquidity ($100 still sells at par), or a possible depeg ($100 sells off too). Alloy members are listed once within 5 points of their static limit. IBC clients are listed once more than half their trusting period has passed without an update.
